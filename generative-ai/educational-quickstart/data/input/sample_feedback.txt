I recently tried the new SmartHome AI Assistant app, and I have to say — it genuinely surprised me.
The voice recognition was nearly flawless, even in a noisy kitchen environment. Setting timers,
adding items to a shopping list, and controlling smart lights all worked on the first try.

However, the app struggles when requests become complex. Asking it to "remind me tomorrow at 9am
to call the dentist if it's raining" resulted in a confused response rather than a conditional
reminder. The natural language understanding for multi-step commands still needs work.

The user interface is clean and intuitive — a first-time user would have no trouble navigating
the main features. The settings menu, though, is buried three levels deep and lacks a search
function, which is frustrating when you need to adjust less obvious options.

Battery consumption while the app runs in the background is noticeably higher than competitors.
Over a full workday, I lost about 12% more battery compared to using the phone without the
assistant running.

Overall rating: 4 out of 5 stars. The core functionality is excellent, the voice recognition
is best-in-class, and the integration with other apps is seamless. Addressing the complex command
handling and battery efficiency would make this a top-tier product.
