# ── Chatbot Prompts ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What is artificial intelligence and how is it different from traditional software?
Explain machine learning to someone who has never heard the term before.
What is the difference between supervised and unsupervised learning?
Write a short poem about creativity and technology working together.
Give me three real-world examples of AI being used in everyday life.
What are large language models and how do they generate text?
Explain neural networks using a simple analogy.
